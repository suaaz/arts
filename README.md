# ARTs — Masterpieces & Their Stories

A minimalist, museum-curated digital exhibition presenting **108 of the world's most famous paintings**, the stories behind their creation, high-resolution artist portraits, years of creation, and extensive historical references.

Designed with an intentional **white theme**, refined museum editorial typography, and distraction-free presentation where the art takes center stage.

---

## 🎨 Featured Masterpieces & In-Depth Stories (108 Works)

Every artwork in the collection includes:
- **Ultra-Clear High-Definition Scans**: 1600px+ museum scans with progressive loading, deep-zoom inspection (1x, 2x, 3x), and direct uncompressed high-resolution view links.
- **The Artist**: Clear portrait or self-portrait of the master, lifespan, nationality, movement, and curatorial profile.
- **The Year Created**: Exact year or creation timeframe.
- **The Story Behind the Masterpiece**: The personal struggles, technical breakthroughs, hidden symbolism, and motivations that led to the painting.
- **Historical References & Context**: The sociopolitical climate, historical events, patrons, and cultural shifts during which the canvas was produced.
- **Museum Placard Data**: Medium, dimensions, and current public collection / museum location.
- **Curatorial Insights**: Memorable historical details and discoveries.

## 🏛️ Design Philosophy

- **Strict Minimalism**: Nothing flashy, no neon gradients, no jarring animations. The interface recedes so the art and story shine.
- **White Theme**: Crisp gallery white `#FFFFFF` backdrop with whisper-soft off-white `#FAFAFA` sections and hairline dividers.
- **Editorial Typography**: Classic serif ([Playfair Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display)) paired with crisp modern sans-serif ([Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)).
- **Dual Exhibition Views**:
  - **Stories Mode**: Expansive editorial monograph with progressive batch loading, artist profile card, deep story, and historical context.
  - **Wall Mode**: Full 108-piece museum exhibition grid for rapid scanning and jumping directly to any canvas.
- **High-Definition Lightbox Inspector**: Fullscreen viewer with multi-level zoom (1x, 2x, 3x) and direct links to uncompressed museum scans.
- **Instant Search & Era Filtering**: Filter across 108 paintings instantly by era or search keywords.
